Methodology matters

“Disabled” is not measured identically in every dataset. The Family Resources Survey uses the core Equality Act 2010 definition: a physical or mental impairment with a substantial and long-term negative effect on normal daily activities. Labour-market publications use survey classifications aligned to the Equality Act definition.

Figures shown here keep their original geographic and time coverage. For example, the PIP figure is England and Wales, while Family Resources Survey headline prevalence is UK-wide. Percentages should therefore not be combined as if they came from one population.
